Cookie Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In shortThis site sets no cookies of its own and uses no tracking. The only outside content that may use cookies is the YouTube video and the map, and both load only after you click them.

Cookies this site sets

None. The site does not set cookies of its own, does not use analytics or advertising cookies, and does not store anything in your browser to track you. That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to opt in to on arrival.

Your choices

Simply do not click play or load the map and no third-party embed contacts its provider from this site. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a content blocker. Blocking third-party fonts may change how the page looks.

Changes and contact

If I add analytics or other tools later, I will update this page and add a consent choice before any non-essential cookie is used. Questions: Contact.superballack@gmail.com.
